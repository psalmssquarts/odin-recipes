Best Guacamole


This guacamole gets added flavors and bright notes from
fresh lemon juice and chopped onion.

Ingredients

  • 2 ovacados
  • Half lemon,juiced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped onion
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • half teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Cut avocados into halves. Remove seeds and scoop out the pulp into a small
    bowl. Use a fork to mash the avocado.
  2. Stir in lemon juice, onion, olive oil, and salt. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for 1
    hour before serving.
